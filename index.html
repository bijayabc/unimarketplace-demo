What it does

Everything a campus marketplace needs

🎓

.edu Verification

Sign up with your college email. OTP verification ensures only real students can access the platform.

🏪

Campus-Scoped Feed

Every listing you see is from a student at your school. No noise from other campuses.

💬

In-App Messaging

Chat directly with buyers or sellers without sharing personal contact info.

📦

Post Listings

Upload photos, set a price, pick a category and condition — live in under a minute.

🔍

Search & Filter

Filter by category, condition, and price. Sort low to high or high to low.

📦

Move-Out Mode

End-of-semester bulk selling mode for students clearing out their dorms fast.
